03 / More than a joke

We know what would have to come next.

FIRST

Private verification

Keep sensitive documents private. Review authenticity and avoid publicly exposing bills or account numbers.

THEN

Safer payment rails

Explore biller-directed payments and a regulated processing partner; never treat pledges as settled bills.

ALWAYS

Transparent status

Clear pending, approved, funded, and paid states with evidence that actual payments completed.
