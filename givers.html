HOW THE REAL VERSION COULD WORK

Clarity before
the first dollar.

We're validating this process with potential assistance and payment partners. None of these money-moving steps are live.

  1. Private bill review and confirmation of the obligation
  2. Supporter chooses a verified type of expense
  3. Approved partner handles funds and pays the biller
  4. Biller settlement is checked before reporting impact

We need your help to validate trust

Giving should feel specific.
And accountable.

These are the safeguards we want to test with real Givers. We won't advertise them as operational until they are verified.

01 / VERIFY

Know it's a real bill.

Documents stay private. A trained reviewer and approved partner confirm the bill before it's eligible for help.

02 / DIRECT

Pay the right place.

Our goal is a partner-managed payment to the named biller, instead of unrestricted transfers to strangers.

03 / CONFIRM

Show the outcome.

A payment should count as completed only after settlement can be confirmed, not when someone swipes or makes a pledge.

Questions worth asking

Before anybody contributes.

Can I give money through OnlyBills today?

No. OnlyBills is a concept preview. We cannot accept or transfer contributions yet. This page collects optional interest only.

Can I choose a particular person or bill?

We are still testing whether support should be allocated by verified bill, expense category, or through an independent assistance partner. The permitted options will depend on payment, partner and legal requirements.

What would a business sponsorship look like?

A potential partner could sponsor a capped, approved assistance challenge. Funding, matching rules, eligibility and tax treatment must be confirmed before any program launches.

Will people in need have to publish their bills?

No. We plan to keep original documents private and publish only a limited, approved summary when consent and safeguards allow.

Is signing up a pledge?

No. Your answers are research interest only. There is no checkout, contract, donation or payment authorization.
