02 / A simple plan

Three things you can do today.

Use this quick checklist to organize your next steps. Nothing you check is sent to OnlyBills or saved to an account.

Ask whether there's a hardship program, payment extension, budget billing, or a payment plan with terms you can afford.

Follow an official link above or contact 211, and ask which programs currently accept applications.

Save reference numbers, eligibility guidance and deadlines privately. Don't post full bills or private account details publicly.

If the situation is urgent

If you're facing imminent eviction, a utility shutoff or another urgent need, reach out to local service organizations and the biller directly. OnlyBills is not an emergency service.

Protect your information
