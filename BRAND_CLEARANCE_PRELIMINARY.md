# OnlyBills — Brand and distribution risk review
Prepared: October 31, 2026
Status: Preliminary market-name scan, NOT legal trademark clearance

## Existing public uses
1. https://onlybills.co/ — site for a similar concept: users post bills; supporters swipe and pay a personal Monzo/PayPal/Revolut link. Site says a human checks requests, then money goes directly between people without checking final use. Very close to our product category, same name.
2. https://onlybills.app/ — unrelated restaurant-receipt splitting and repayment app; public website and App Store listing. Also uses the identical name and has an iOS release history dating to Dec 2025.
3. https://onlybills.netlify.app/ — unrelated invoice-generator site using the same name.
4. https://onlybillss.netlify.app/ — our fictional swipe demo, Giver interview interest list and planned partner-led direct-to-biller assistance. No bill payments currently live.

## Meaning
- A suffix/domain distinction (.co, .app, .netlify.app) does NOT establish a distinct trademark for similar services.
- The similar onlybills.co site creates a plausible marketplace-confusion risk that is materially stronger than a distinct-vertical name collision.
- The USPTO recommends a comprehensive clearance search including federal pending/registered marks, state databases, relevant common-law marketplace use and potentially other jurisdictions. See:
  https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks
  https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion
- This is NOT proof of infringement or priority; our browsing did not establish any claimant's date of first U.S. commercial use, registration status, jurisdiction, or ownership.
- Do not accuse anyone of copying, claim we invented the meme, or tell users that we own exclusive rights to "OnlyBills."

## Go/no-go gate before brand investment
[ ] Run trademark database searches (exact and phonetic: OnlyBills, Only Bills, BillHelp, variants), inspect real statuses, goods/services, owners and filing dates.
[ ] Search Rhode Island and relevant state business/trademark filings and discoverable marketplace uses.
[ ] Investigate the similarly-named bill-help competitor's entity, location, operating dates, country and actual services without contacting them to make allegations.
[ ] Have a U.S. trademark attorney assess U.S. use, related services, likelihood of confusion, and whether an alternate brand is advisable.
[ ] Decide whether to keep OnlyBills, adopt a more distinctive house brand, or choose a new name. Get permission before changing the user-facing brand, domain, logo, and social handles.
[ ] Delay paying for trademarks, domain acquisitions, branding or ads until that decision; retain all project code and audience data privately.

## Proposed differentiation (a future promise, NOT a live service)
"Know the bill was paid." Independent bill verification, payment to actual biller through an approved partner, and verifiable settlement events.
These must remain *planned* until a financial partner is approved, contracts signed, and real biller confirmations verified.

## Acquisition experiment
- Direct individuals and local businesses to /givers, then an in-page, consent-based *interview* form, not a payments checkout.
- No application for aid or donation authorization.
- Use a partner-led RI utility assistance pilot as the narrowest initial model. The official Good Neighbor Energy Fund operates verified energy aid and pays utility companies directly:
  https://www.unitedwayri.org/gnef/
- Treat interview readiness and requested follow-up as meaningful qualification, not binding sponsorship.
- Review submissions daily for two weeks; exclude tests and duplicates.
- Avoid fundraising or implying charitable tax deductibility without partner/legal clearance.
